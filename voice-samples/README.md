# Narrator previews

Ana was selected for the game: a light American child narrator at 25% below the voice's normal speaking rate. These previews use original synthetic narration.

- `ana-slow.mp3`: Ana, American English child voice.
- `maisie-slow.mp3`: Maisie, British English child voice.
- `libby-slow.mp3`: Libby, British English female voice.

Each sample says: “Hello! I am Thomas. Tap right to go. Tap left to stop. Space makes a peep.”

Voice classifications are listed in [Microsoft's speech voice catalogue](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/language-support?tabs=tts). The samples and game's embedded narration were generated with [edge-tts](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts). Generating new clips requires internet access; playing the finished game works offline.
